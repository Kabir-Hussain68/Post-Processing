# Post-Processing
My Post-Processing filter for images

## Disclaimer
These filters are for images only, they are not particularly optimized. These post-processing effects serve only as a showcase and are made purely for educational purposes. My inspiration for these was the post-processing series on Youtube made by Acerola for Unity.<br>
To plug in your custom images, first put the images in `assets\texture` and then go into `main.cpp` and plug in the path of your image `Texture image = loadTexture("assets/textures/images.jpg")` <br>
To export a image just simply click on the export button and the image should be exported in `export\images.png`<br>
**Note :** Image drop down will be supported in the future (hopefully)

## Features
- Difference of Gaussians
  - Basic
  - Extended
